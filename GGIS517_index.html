The Tunnel and Reservoir Plan (TARP), also known as the Chicago Deep Tunnel, is a large-scale infrastructure system designed to reduce flooding and improve water quality in the Chicago region.

Learn more on official website

TARP helps prevent flooding by capturing excess stormwater and sewage during heavy rainfall. The water is temporarily stored underground and treated later, instead of overflowing into streets and rivers.
